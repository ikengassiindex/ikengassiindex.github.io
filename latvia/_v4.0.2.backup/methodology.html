Methodology

How the SSI v4.0.2 is calculated — from raw data to final classification. Open methodology, open data, fully reproducible.

Breaking the Silos

Power grid resilience is inseparable from its socio-economic context. A NUTS-3 Region serving an energy-poor region with slow restoration times and weak fiscal capacity is fundamentally more vulnerable than one with identical electrical characteristics in a prosperous area. The SSI captures this reality by fusing traditional grid metrics with socio-economic, environmental, and energy-transition data — all from public sources.

Processing Pipeline
1

Ingest

95 variables from 30+ verified public data sources — AST Avaandmed, SPRK, Sadales tīkls, CSP (Centrālā statistikas pārvalde), Latvijas Banka, LVĢMC (Latvijas Vides, ģeoloģijas un meteoroloģijas centrs), LĢIA (Latvijas Ģeotelpiskās informācijas aģentūra), LVĢMC (LVĢMC-Klimats), EFEHR ESHM20, ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, Nord Pool EE bidding zone, Copernicus ERA5, OpenStreetMap Overpass, Eurostat NUTS-3, and others. Zero proprietary SCADA dependencies. Maximum ingestion frequency: weekly.

2

Normalise

Four normalisation methods: Method A (robust fleet percentile P5/P95), Method B (standard fleet percentile), Method C (bounded rescaling), and Method D (categorical mapping). Inverted metrics for density measures where higher = better resilience.

3

Weight

6-level hierarchical weighting across 6 components and 20 metrics. Continuity dominates at 0.30, followed by Infrastructure (0.25) and Saturation (0.20). Weight budget validated by Sobol sensitivity analysis.

4

Compose R_base

Weighted sum of 6 normalised component scores produces the base resilience score.

5

Modify

Six multiplicative modifiers adjust R_base for context: R2 (adaptive climate IRI), R3 (consequence + energy poverty), R4 (graph-theoretic network criticality), R6a (restoration speed), R6b (network topology), R7 (digital readiness proxy). Plus 5 micro-enrichments for vulnerable populations, flood zones, landslides, digital capabilities, and healthcare criticality.

6

Monte Carlo

10,000 iterations per NUTS-3 Region using a 20×20 Gaussian copula correlation matrix. Captures measurement, spatial, and staleness uncertainty. Produces median, P5, P95, skewness, and P_critical for each NUTS-3 Region.

7

Classify

Four bands: Low (0.00–0.25), Medium (0.25–0.50), High (0.50–0.75), Critical (0.75–1.00). Alert flags trigger when any single component exceeds its P95 fleet threshold.

Master Equation

v4.0.2
R_final = soft_clip_upper( R_base × F_topo × C_mult × R6a_mult × R6b_net × Cyber_factor ) R_base = 0.30·C + 0.10·V + 0.25·I + 0.10·E + 0.20·S + 0.05·T F_topo = graph_criticality(degree, BC, bridge) // R4 — [0.80, 1.35] C_mult = consequence_sigmoid(pop, load, V_socio) // R3 — [0.70, 1.30] R6a_mult = restoration_sigmoid(CAIDI_local) // R6a — [0.90, 1.10] R6b_net = network_topology(centrality, ring) // R6b — [1.00, 1.25] Cyber = desi_digital_index(region, voltage) // R7 — [0.99, 1.05]

Modifiers R2–R7 (incl. R6a + R6b)

Context multipliers
ModifierDomainRangeWhat It Captures
R2 Adaptive IRI + Climate internal Shifts weight from climate-IRI to structural metrics where local climate risk is low. CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 projections adjust forward-looking risk.
R3 Consequence + Poverty [0.70, 1.30] Amplifies risk for NUTS-3 Region serving large/energy-poor populations with high economic dependency. Includes fiscal, demographic, elderly, and flood enrichments.
R4 Graph Criticality [0.80, 1.35] Penalises topological bottlenecks: high betweenness centrality, bridge nodes, low degree. Built from OSM power graph.
R6a Restoration Speed [0.90, 1.10] SPRK-CAIDI-based: rewards fast-restoring areas, penalises slow ones. Two NUTS-3 Region with identical SAIDI can have different risk profiles.
R6b Network Topology [1.00, 1.25] Network centrality and ring topology. Penalises NUTS-3 Region in single-source or low-redundancy configurations. Based on physical network analysis.
R7 Digital Readiness [0.99, 1.05] Regional DESI digital readiness score, smart meter penetration proxy, and HV voltage class bonus. Indicates cyber-resilience capability.

Uncertainty Quantification

Monte Carlo engine

Every NUTS-3 Region score includes a 90% confidence interval derived from 10,000 Monte Carlo iterations. The simulation uses a 20×20 Gaussian copula to preserve empirical correlations between metrics (e.g., ρ(C1, E1) ≈ 0.70–0.85). Three uncertainty sources are modelled: measurement error, spatial disaggregation noise, and data staleness.
