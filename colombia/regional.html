T1 · Capital-Metro / Industrial
Bogotá D.C.

Capital district + Distrito Capital special administrative status. Population ~7.9M (DANE 2024); ~16% of national GDP; government + financial services + tertiary core. R3 ceiling 1.05. Codensa S.A. E.S.P. (Enel Colombia) is the regional DSO with ~30% of national MWh. Critical supply: Guavio 1,200 MW (Emgesa, Cundinamarca-Boyacá border) is the reservoir-electricity nexus anchor for Bogotá D.C. supply continuity during dry-season El Niño years.

T1 · Capital-Metro / Industrial
Antioquia (Medellín)

Medellín industrial heartland + Aburrá Valley manufacturing concentration. Population ~6.9M; GDP/cap ~$8k. EPM (Empresa Pública de Medellín) is the municipally-owned 100%-City-of-Medellín DSO + dominant hydro generator. EPM hydro portfolio includes Guatapé + San Carlos 1,240 MW + Porce I-II-III-IV + Ituango 2,400 MW (cohort-largest hydro project; partial commissioning 2018-2024, full commissioning 2024 added ~1,200 MW). R6_drought α 0.15-0.20 — cohort-highest hydro-dependence exposure.

T4 · Pacific / Frontier
Chocó (Quibdó)

Pacific rainforest cohort-poorest departamento. Population ~0.55M; GDP/cap ~$2-3k (cohort-low). Wettest place on earth (~10,000 mm/yr precipitation). V_socio baseline 0.20 — cohort-lowest social-resilience capacity. Significant Afro-Colombian + Embera indigenous population. R6_armed_conflict residual exposure (Clan del Golfo + ELN). R6_flood α 0.18 — cohort-highest flood-landslide exposure. DSO: DISPAC with long radial 34.5 kV feeds and structural quality-of-supply gap vs Andean corridor.
