Systemic System Infrastructure Index — The First Open Digital Twin Fusing Grid Risk with Societal Exposure

A composite resilience score for Colombia's electrical grid — 381 substations across 32 departamentos plus Bogotá D.C., scored against the XM CND dispatch + ISA INTERCOLOMBIA 500/230/220/138 kV STN backbone and the fragmented ~25-DSO distribution market (Codensa Enel + EPM municipal + EPSA/Celsia + Air-e/Afinia + 20+ regional). 95 variables, 30+ public data sources, zero proprietary dependencies. XM system operation since June 1995 + CREG tariff regulation + MME/UPME planning + ColCERT/CSIRT-XM cyber oversight. Population ~52.2M (DANE 2024); 37th OECD member (accession 28 April 2020 — first Andean OECD member, second South American after Chile 2010).

Operated as a non-profit through a dedicated foundation — ensuring the Index serves the public interest.
Breaking the Silos

Power grid resilience is inseparable from its socio-economic context — and in Colombia, inseparable from its hydrology. The SSI fuses traditional grid metrics with socio-economic, environmental, and energy-transition data — all from public sources (XM, CREG, DANE, IDEAM, SGC, Unidad de Víctimas) — to capture what single-discipline indices miss: the 1992-1993 Apagón rationing, the El Niño reservoir-electricity nexus, the Nevado del Ruiz 1985 Armero anchor, and the durable R6_armed_conflict layer that ELN + dissidente FARC + Clan del Golfo still place on pylons and substations.

Cohort-First Modifiers

Colombia introduces two NEW modifiers to the SSI cohort: R6_drought in its hydro-reservoir-drawdown form (distinct from Israel's desalination-electricity nexus form — same field, different α-mapping logic), and R6_armed_conflict, the first OECD-cohort modifier capturing durable substation-proximate infrastructure-targeting.
