A tailored offer for Vísir.

A free, monthly or quarterly, co-branded Ikenga + SSI.Index country report — built for the readers of Vísir.

The SSI Index is the first open, auditable measure of substation vulnerability across the OECD. It fuses climate, seismic, infrastructure, economic, demographic, energy-transition risk — and, from v4.6, a new Human dimension that translates the score into the actual community consequence of a failure: life-support load, cold-chain clinical nodes, severe energy poverty, services that are now digital-only.

The result: every substation has a name, a municipality, a served catchment. Its fragility becomes a verifiable story, not an abstraction.
