Environmental Pillar

Climate resilience & hazard exposure

Denmark's electrical infrastructure faces intensifying North Sea storm hazards, coastal flooding, and wind loading stress. The SSI's R6b modifier (North Sea storm + storm surge + wind loading overlay, [1.00, 1.25] range) captures this systemic exposure. 40% of substations lie within the 50-year storm surge projection zone. Energinet's climate adaptation roadmap explicitly targets the Great Belt corridor and coastal transmission hubs identified as Critical (R ≥ 0.75) by the SSI.

Social Pillar

Consequence & community resilience

The R3 consequence modifier (consequence + island isolation + population density, [0.70, 1.30] range) reveals social vulnerability concentrated in three zones: Copenhagen metropolitan (largest load centre), island communities (Bornholm, Fanø, Laesø — isolated topology), and rural heating-dependent regions (district heating CHP correlation). The SSI flags 180–220 substations serving populations ≥30% energy-poor or with >60% district heating dependency as High/Critical Risk.

Governance Pillar

Transparency, digital readiness, regulatory alignment

The R7 digital readiness modifier (regulatory cyber-physical baseline, smart meter penetration proxy, HV voltage class bonus, [0.99, 1.05] range) measures governance maturity. Denmark ranks highest in OECD for regulatory transparency via Forsyningstilsynet's open reporting mandate. The SSI v4.0.2 operates under full open-source methodology, zero proprietary dependencies, and live GitHub issue tracking. All 95 variables are auditable against public source documentation.

Data Sources

Climate & Hazard: DMI (North Sea storm data, 40-year hindcast), GEUS (coastal geology, storm surge projections), Copernicus (satellite hazard monitoring).
