ESG Framework Overview

6 ESG dimensions aligned to UN SDGs

This substation-level ESG report maps the SSI Index's 6 components and 5 modifiers to environmental, social, and governance dimensions aligned with CSRD/ESRS Article 8, TCFD recommendations, and UN Sustainable Development Goals. Each dimension includes materiality assessment, readiness scoring, and traceability to source data.

Environmental (E)

Infrastructure resilience to climate hazards (IRI), asset condition degradation (Markov), and transition readiness. Mapped to SSI components I (Infrastructure), V (Voltage Quality), T (Transition).

Social (S)

Socio-economic consequence, population served, vulnerability to outages, community dependence on electricity. Mapped to SSI R3 modifier and E (Economic) component.

Governance (G)

Digital readiness, cyber-physical resilience, operational reliability, network topology criticality. Mapped to SSI R7 (Digital), R4 (Graph Criticality), C (Continuity).

Materiality

Weighted across 95 variables and 20 metrics. Substation-level context (voltage, region, asset age, restoration speed) drives material factor assignment.

Methodology & Data Traceability

ESG Framework: The SSI Index v4.0.2 combines 95 variables from 30+ public data sources into 6 weighted components and 5 context-specific modifiers. This report extracts substation-level ESG metrics mapped to CSRD Annex I, ESRS E1/S1/G1, and TCFD frameworks. All calculations are reproducible; source code and data lineage are publicly available on GitHub.
