SSI Engine

Server-side canonical engine

The SSI v4.2 canonical scoring engine is scripts/pipeline/scoring/engine.py (Python, numpy-vectorized). It implements the complete formula construct: 6 component weights, 20 metric intra-weights, all 11 modifier functions (5 v4.0.2 baseline — R3 consequence sigmoid, R4 graph criticality, R6a restoration speed, R6b network topology, R7 digital readiness — plus 6 v4.2 resilience family: R6c flood additive cliff [+0.00, +0.30], R6d wildfire FWI [1.00, 1.20], R6e winter Bourgouin freezing-rain p99 [1.00, 1.15], R8 adaptive capacity, R9 compound pairwise coupling, R10 energy justice), the Re composite aggregate (bounds [0.920, 1.787]), T1 DER stress composite, normalisation methods, soft-clip overflow compression with v4.2 additive shift OUTSIDE the clip, and a 10,000-iteration Monte Carlo (20×20 Gaussian copula via Cholesky decomposition). v4.2 master equation: R_final = soft_clip_upper(R_base × Π mult_i) + Σ (add_i − 1.0). The browser-side ssi-engine.js engine is retired (PR-4, audit memo 2026-06-08); it now reads precomputed R_median, R_P5, R_P95, CI_width, P_critical, skewness, Re_raw, Re_norm, modifier_impacts directly from ssi-data.json.
