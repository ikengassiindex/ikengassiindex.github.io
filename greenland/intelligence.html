The SSI Index's Greenland deployment is being bootstrapped from publicly-verifiable geometry before any R scores are computed. The fleet entering the first scoring session comprises 36 substations, 74 plants / generators and 125 power-line segments — every asset geolocated from OpenStreetMap (extract 2026-04-16), cross-validated against Nukissiorfiit's published transmission / distribution coverage map, and with Pituffik Space Base (U.S. Navy / Space Force / Air Force operator, lat 75.5°–77.5°N, lon −71°…−66°) excluded by policy (see methodology §7). No synthetic nodes, no inferred cross-settlement edges: Greenland's grid is a set of isolated within-island networks and the topology reflects that exactly.

Regional distribution of the substation fleet: Kujalleq 20 (56%), Qeqertalik 12 (33%), Qeqqata 3 (8%), Sermersooq 1 (3%). Avannaata has 16 plants and 18 line segments in the OSM extract but zero tagged substations — a known coverage gap that will either be closed by Nukissiorfiit direct ingest at Session 1 or logged as a permanent data limitation. Because N = 36 sits well below the standard v4.0.2 operating regime (N > 500), the §8 Small-N Guardrail is active: the R3 tier-balance rule is relaxed from 5–25% to 2–50% per tier, and the copula Monte Carlo propagation is deferred to the July session when a calibrated correlation matrix is available.

Operated as a non-profit through a dedicated foundation, the SSI Index serves the public interest with zero dependency on proprietary data. All 95 variables are drawn from 25+ verified public sources (Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur, Grønlands Statistik, Asiaq, GEUS, OpenStreetMap, CMIP6, Open-Meteo). Of those, 8 fields are live today (geometry, operator, region, voltage_kv, graph_topology.degree, seismic.PGA_g, coordinate, name) and 87 remain pending until Session 1 ingests Nukissiorfiit SCADA, tariff, and reliability feeds. This is a deliberately narrow launch surface — methodology first, numbers second.

Section C — Recurring Module

Cyber & Economic Exposure Monitor

Why this section exists: Traditional grid risk frameworks treat cybersecurity and economic vulnerability as separate domains. The SSI's R7 (Digital Readiness) modifier and E (Economic) component cross these silos — identifying substations where low digital resilience intersects with high socio-economic consequence. This is precisely the anticipatory intelligence that Nukissiorfiit and Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur strategic planning will require.

Section E

Deep-Dive: The Buksefjord Corridor

The Buksefjord hydropower complex (45 MW, commissioned 1993, expanded 2007) feeding Nuuk via a 132 kV submarine-plus-overhead transmission is the most strategically critical single asset in Greenland's grid. It is the only Greenlandic installation where R4 graph criticality can plausibly rise into the Critical band under scoring — Nuuk has no realistic alternative supply path and any Buksefjord interruption places the capital on full diesel backup. At the same time, its Atlantic-facing corridor exposes the transmission right-of-way to polar-low storms, ice accretion, and freezing salt spray — hazards that the R6a restoration modifier (α ∈ [0.55, 0.90]) and the R6b Arctic storm overlay are calibrated to capture.

Because Sermersooq only contains 1 OSM-tagged substation in the current bootstrap — Buksefjord itself or its immediate downstream node — the SSI can't yet quantify corridor-wide cascade risk. The Session 1 ingest will rely on Nukissiorfiit to supply the complete Nuuk-ring topology (Qinngorput, Nuussuaq, downtown, and the Qaquliaasaq tie) before the corridor deep-dive can be replaced with a live R4 + R6b heat-band analysis. For Edition 000 this section is a methodological marker: the corridor is where the R6a Arctic restoration calibration and the §7 Pituffik exclusion policy were originally stress-tested.

Data Architecture — Component Taxonomy

How the SSI's 6 components and 20 sub-metrics are sourced, validated and scored for Greenland's grid

The SSI-ENN's Layer 1 formally specifies the SSI v4.0.2 Index for Greenland — its six components (Continuity, Voltage, Infrastructure, Economic, Saturation, Transition), twenty sub-metrics, normalisation methodology and aggregation rules. Each sub-metric is independently sourced from one of the 25+ public data sources in the Greenlandic SSI registry (Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur, Grønlands Statistik, Asiaq, GEUS), validated against known reference distributions, and normalised to a common [0, 1] scale before aggregation. For Edition 000 the registry is in place and the scoring engine is wired up; what's missing is the first ingest pass. Session 1 closes that gap.

Key Concepts
→6 components, 20 sub-metrics, 95 variables from 25+ Greenlandic sources
→Min-max normalisation with winsorised tails
→Component weights: 0.30·C + 0.10·V + 0.25·I + 0.10·E + 0.20·S + 0.05·T
→Each sub-metric traceable to Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur or an open data source

The Greenlandic training set comprises 36 substations with 8-of-95 feature vectors populated today; the remaining 87 fields will arrive at Session 1. That bootstrap is intentionally small: N = 36 is well below the ~250-substation regime the engine normally operates in, which is why the §8 Small-N Guardrail (R3 tier-balance 2–50%) and the Monte Carlo copula deferral are active. First Monte Carlo runs — 10,000 iterations per substation through the 20×20 Gaussian copula — will produce the first live R_median, P5 / P95 bounds and CI widths in Edition 001.

Section H

Looking Ahead to Session 1 (2026-07)

Session 1 gate. The FIRST_REFRESH = 2026-07 monthly-cadence gate intentionally suppresses April, May and June scoring runs. This avoids publishing half-filled R scores that would be overwritten twice before the full Nukissiorfiit ingest lands. In July 2026 the scoring engine will execute its first end-to-end pass: geometry ✓ (live today) → raw ingest → normalisation → component rollup → modifier stack (R3 / R4 / R6a / R6b / R7) → Monte Carlo (10,000 iterations × 36 substations = 360,000 draws through the 20×20 Gaussian copula) → R_median, P5 / P95 bounds, classification band, CI width → publication.

Session 1 priorities. (1) Nukissiorfiit transmission + distribution SCADA ingest (populates C1–C4 and R6a). (2) Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur Afbrydelsestatistik 2024 (populates SAIDI reference and R3 consequence multiplier). (3) GEUS bedrock + permafrost feed (I3, R6b ice-accretion). (4) CMIP6 Arctic regional downscaling (R6b polar-low storm, freezing-spray, surge). (5) Asiaq reanalysis weather feed (I5 thermal). (6) Avannaata substation backfill — the only OSM coverage gap that materially affects fleet-level metrics. (7) First SSI-ENN Layer 2 valuation outputs, anchored to Buksefjord and Sisimiut hydro.

What this edition establishes. A verified 36-substation / 74-plant / 125-line bootstrap fleet, Pituffik correctly excluded, the §8 Small-N Guardrail documented, the §7 exclusion policy logged in meta.pituffik_excluded, and the methodology §7 and §8 additions published. From this foundation, every subsequent edition becomes a delta against a known baseline — the exact transparency standard the SSI is built to maintain.
