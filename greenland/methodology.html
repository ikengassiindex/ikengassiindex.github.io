Methodology

How the SSI v4.0.2 is calculated — from raw data to final classification. Open methodology, open data, fully reproducible. Greenland-specific annex (§7 Pituffik, §8 Small-N guardrail, Arctic modifier calibration) applies on top of the pan-OECD base.

Breaking the Silos

Power grid resilience is inseparable from its socio-economic context. A substation serving an energy-poor kommune with slow restoration times and weak fiscal capacity is fundamentally more vulnerable than one with identical electrical characteristics in a prosperous area. The SSI captures this reality by fusing traditional grid metrics with socio-economic, environmental, and energy-transition data — all from public sources.

Processing Pipeline
1

Ingest

95 variables from 30+ verified Greenlandic public data sources — Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur, Grønlands Statistik, Asiaq, GEUS, Asiaq, CFCS, OSM, Copernicus, and others. Zero proprietary SCADA dependencies. Max frequency: real-time (Nukissiorfiit dispatch).

2

Normalise

Four normalisation methods: Method A (robust fleet percentile P5/P95), Method B (standard fleet percentile), Method C (bounded rescaling), and Method D (categorical mapping). Inverted metrics for density measures where higher = better resilience.

3

Weight

6-level hierarchical weighting across 6 components and 20 metrics. Continuity at 0.30, Infrastructure at 0.25 (with higher Arctic polar-low storm/ice accretion weight), Saturation at 0.20. Weight budget validated by Sobol sensitivity analysis.

4

Compose R_base

Weighted sum of 6 normalised component scores produces the base resilience score.

5

Modify

Six multiplicative modifiers adjust R_base for context: R2 (adaptive climate IRI — Arctic polar-low storm focus), R3 (consequence + island isolation + population density), R4 (graph-theoretic network criticality + Buksefjord bottleneck), R6a (restoration speed), R6b (Arctic polar-low storm + polar-low surge + ice accretion overlay — CRITICAL FOR GREENLAND), R7 (digital readiness proxy). Plus 5 micro-enrichments for vulnerable populations, flood zones, Arctic polar-low storm weather, energy transition, and healthcare criticality.

6

Monte Carlo

10,000 iterations per substation using a 95-dimensional Gaussian copula. Captures measurement, spatial, and staleness uncertainty. Wider σ for older hydro-served and coastal infrastructure. Produces median, P5, P95, skewness, and P_critical for each substation.

7

Classify

Four bands: Low (0.00–0.25), Medium (0.25–0.50), High (0.50–0.75), Critical (0.75–1.00). Alert flags trigger when any single component exceeds its P95 fleet threshold.

Uncertainty Quantification

Monte Carlo engine

Every substation score includes a 90% confidence interval derived from 10,000 Monte Carlo iterations. The simulation uses a 95-dimensional Gaussian copula to preserve empirical correlations between metrics (e.g., ρ(North_Sea_Storm, Wind_Loading) ≈ 0.72 in Greenlandic coastal zones). Three uncertainty sources are modelled: measurement error, spatial disaggregation noise, and data staleness. Wider σ applied for older hydro-served and exposed coastal infrastructure.

§7   Pituffik Space Base — Exclusion

Avannaata Kommune

Pituffik Space Base (formerly Thule Air Base) is a U.S. Space Force installation in northern Avannaata Kommune. It operates an independent electrical grid outside of Nukissiorfiit's scope, with asset data that is not publicly available. In line with the SSI's public-data-only policy, all Pituffik assets are excluded from ingestion, scoring, reporting and fleet summaries.

Implementation: the OSM ingestion query strips any node inside the geographic box lat ∈ [75.5°, 77.5°], lon ∈ [−71°, −66°], as well as any asset whose operator tag references the U.S. Navy, U.S. Space Force, U.S. Air Force or Pituffik itself. The exclusion is logged in ssi-data.json · meta.pituffik_excluded = true and echoed on the index, data and methodology pages.

Related exclusions: Qulliq Energy Corporation (Nunavut), Hydro-Québec, RARIK and Veitur (Iceland) — filtered out at ingestion when the initial OSM bounding box incidentally captured neighbouring territories. See the Data Ingestion Audit for the full exclusion list.

§8   Small-N Guardrail & First-Refresh Gate

N=37 · Session 1 scored 2026-04-16
§8.1 R3 tier-balance relaxation

Standard pan-OECD policy requires every band (Low / Medium / High / Critical) to hold 5–45% of the fleet. At N=36 this is arithmetically impossible. For small-N entities (N<500) the bounds relax to 2–50% per band. The rule is retained as default; the relaxation is logged against each violated band with reason small_N_relaxation.

§8.2 Monte-Carlo — reduced iterations, no copula

At N=37 the canonical 10,000-iteration MC is reduced to 1,000 per substation (37,000 total draws) to avoid over-fitting variance structure on a small fleet. The full 95-dimensional Gaussian copula is held off — components are perturbed independently — because empirical correlation matrices inferred from 37 observations are numerically unstable. Engine fields (R_median, R_P5, R_P95, CI_width, classification, modifier_impact, P_critical) are populated with data_status="OFFICIAL" and edition="001".

§8.3 Graph topology — within-island only

R4 graph criticality is computed per islanded micro-grid. Cross-settlement edges are never synthesised — Greenland has no inter-kommune HV interconnector. Graph degree is LIVE across all 5 Kommuner (avg 0.8 Kujalleq, 1.0 Sermersooq, 0.3 Qeqqata, 0.0 Qeqertalik, 0.0 Avannaata — the Ilulissat transformer is an isolated leaf node).

§8.4 FIRST_REFRESH gate = 2026-04 (brought forward)

Session 1 was brought forward from July to April 2026 once the Arctic/Greenland calibration inputs (Stat.gl 2024/25 socio-economic, Nukissiorfiit 2024 Annual Report CAIDI ≈ 8h and RE share ≈ 70%, Asiaq/DMI climate trajectories, GEUS stable-craton seismic) converged. Standard monthly cadence begins 2026-05. From then on Greenland is bundled with Denmark's archive (ARCHIVE_BUNDLES={"denmark":["greenland"]}).

Edition 001 (scored 2026-04-16): 37 substations across 5 Kommuner · 74 plants / generators · 125 power-line segments · all 95 fields scored · fleet median R = 0.779 · 29 substations in the Critical band.
