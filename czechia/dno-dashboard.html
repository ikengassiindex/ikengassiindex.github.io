3 DSOs · 5.94 M LV customers · 14 kraje

Unlike France's near-mono Enedis market, Czechia's distribution is split across three DSOs with very different operating profiles. Praha (PRE) operates an underground urban network with the lowest SAIDI in the SSI fleet outside of pure city-states; ČEZ Distribuce serves the largest rural footprint; EG.D occupies South Moravia and Vysočina. Why the SAIDI spread? — Network archetype

The 5.3× SAIDI gap between ČEZ-Dist and PREdistribuce is dominated by network archetype, not management quality:

  • PRE — urban underground. 90%+ of MV cabling underground, fault rates 1/10 of overhead. Smart-meter rollout fastest. Praha as a single contiguous urban service area means concentrated maintenance and short response times.
  • EG.D — mixed Moravian basin. Mostly overhead in rural areas; flatter terrain than ČEZ-Dist's mountain regions. Faces the highest seasonal flood exposure (Morava + Dyje basins).
  • ČEZ Distribuce — large mountainous footprint. Ten kraje including Šumava (JHC), Krušné hory (KVK + ULK), Krkonoše (HKK), Beskydy (MSK). Overhead lines in zones with IEC 60826 ice classes 4-5 (highest in CZ). Long restoration times during winter storms drag the average up.

Per Italy Formula Construct §4 R6, the operating-stress modifier is calibrated to penalise these archetypes proportionally — see the modifier R6 column in Intelligence.

SAIDI in OECD context

National Czech SAIDI (79.7 min, weighted by household share) is materially worse than the Western European median but better than Slovakia and Poland. The gap to Germany (12 min) reflects three decades of post-1989 grid replacement still underway, plus the 33% coal generation share which sustains higher-fault transmission infrastructure.
