DSO Segmented Dashboard — Estonia

Near-monopoly DSO market · ~700k LV customers · 15 Maakond · 1 dominant (Elektrilevi ~85%) + small regional + captives

Estonia's distribution market is structurally a monopoly: Elektrilevi OÜ (Eesti Energia subsidiary) serves ~85% of low-voltage customers across most of the country, with three smaller operators covering specific regions or captive industrial loads. The transmission backbone is operated by Elering AS as single TSO (330 / 220 / 110 kV; the 220 kV legacy Russian-era lines are being decommissioned by 2030). Estonia desynchronized from the BRELL ring on 8 February 2025 and is now part of the Continental European synchronous area via LitPol Link + Harmony Link. Per KB §46.3, Markov degradation priors carry a medium confidence tier until 5+ years of Continental-sync data accumulate (2030-02-08 review).

Strategic context — what to watch

Oil-shale phase-out (Ida-Viru): Narva-Auvere power complex historically supplied ~75% of Estonia's electricity. Phasing out 2024–2026 with EU Just Transition Fund support (€354M committed 2021–2027). Elektrilevi's 142-min SAIDI in Ida-Viru reflects under-investment during the phase-out planning cycle — now reversing.

BRELL desync (8 Feb 2025): Frequency-stability data vintage is structurally split. RoCoF events above ±0.5 Hz/s settled from a peak of 36/yr in Q1 2025 to ~18/yr by Q4 2025 (still ~2× mature Continental-sync members). Confidence tier remains medium for Markov priors until 2030-02-08 review.

Saaremaa offshore wind (planned 2027–2030): Saare-Liivi offshore wind park (~250 MW first phase) will require substantial Elering 110 kV reinforcement and a new undersea cable to mainland Lääne — currently the island's single point of failure (110 kV submarine link, Lääne anchor).

Hiiumaa wind (planned 2028): Future ~120 MW wind farm on Hiiumaa requires undersea cable upgrade. Current 1-cable dependency drives Hiiu's 198-min SAIDI — worst in fleet.

R7 cyber posture: Estonia's DESI score (0.82 in 2024) is the highest in the OECD, reflecting e-Estonia digital infrastructure (X-Road, e-residency, electronic ID). For SSI scoring, this means R7 ceiling effect: nearly all EE substations score R7 ≈ 1.05 (the modifier upper bound). Decision recorded in KB §46.6: accept the ceiling for the EE build; revisit if it materially distorts cross-country comparison.
