Section 1

Overview

This dashboard presents a comprehensive site assessment of Greece's 581 electrical substations across 13 Peripheries (administrative regions), operated by two primary entities: ADMIE/IPTO (transmission, ≥150 kV) and DEDDIE/HEDNO (distribution, <150 kV).

The assessment framework evaluates substations using multiple criteria including risk profiles, regional distribution, and operational characteristics to support investment planning and grid resilience strategies.

Section 3

Greek Grid Operators

ADMIE/IPTO (Transmission System Operator)

Operates the high-voltage transmission network at 150 kV and above, managing the backbone of Greece's electrical infrastructure. Responsible for system stability, long-distance power transfer, and interconnection with neighbouring countries.

DEDDIE/HEDNO (Distribution System Operator)

Manages the medium and low-voltage distribution networks (<150 kV), serving as the interface between transmission and end consumers. Operates the local distribution infrastructure across all Greek regions.

Section 4

Assessment Framework

Each substation is assessed across multiple dimensions:

  • Location & Region: Geographic placement and regional context
  • Voltage Level: Classification as transmission or distribution asset
  • Operator Assignment: ADMIE/IPTO or DEDDIE/HEDNO responsibility
  • Risk Profile: Multi-component risk assessment including reliability, voltage stability, infrastructure, and transition readiness
Section 5

Risk Classification

Substations are classified into four risk bands based on comprehensive assessment:

Low Risk
Minimal Intervention Required
Well-maintained assets with strong operational resilience and modern infrastructure.
Medium Risk
Routine Monitoring
Assets requiring standard maintenance and periodic upgrades.
High Risk
Prioritized Investment
Assets requiring near-term capital investment and enhanced monitoring.
Critical Risk
Urgent Action Required
Assets requiring immediate intervention to ensure grid stability and safety.
Section 6

Regional Distribution

Substations are distributed across Greece's 13 Peripheries, with concentration varying by regional demand patterns, transmission backbone density, and historical infrastructure development.
