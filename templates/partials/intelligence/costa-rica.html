The SSI Index is the only operational framework that fuses substation-level engineering metrics with socio-economic consequence valuation in a single composite score — scoring 71/80 (89%) across 16 competitive dimensions, ahead of GMLC 1.1 (48/80), EPRI (40/80), and academic MCDA frameworks (41/80). For Costa Rica, its engine processes 74,000+ source data points per run, executes 35.02 million Monte Carlo simulations through a 20×20 Gaussian copula correlation matrix, and performs 830+ million arithmetic operations — producing 210,000+ output data points with full uncertainty quantification across 169 substations and ~4,261 grid lines.

Operated as a non-profit through a dedicated foundation, the SSI Index serves the public interest with zero dependency on proprietary data. All 95 variables are drawn from 30+ verified public sources — ICE network statement, ARESEP + quality reports, CNFL + ICE distribution + Coopelesca/Coopeguanacaste cooperative filings, INEC regional accounts, BCCR national accounts, IMN consolidated meteorology + hydrology + OVSICORI seismic + volcanic monitoring, MINAE air quality, CSIRT-CR + MICITT (ECOI) cyber posture — making the methodology fully auditable and reproducible. Costa Rica is the inaugural single-country onboarding post-cohort-closure (Session 33B), deployed on the 13 August 2026 anchor as the first OECD founding-member country onboarded since the CEE-South-2026 architectural-acceptance run.

Cyber & Economic Exposure Monitor

Why this section exists: Traditional grid risk frameworks treat cybersecurity and economic vulnerability as separate domains. The SSI's R7 (Digital Readiness) modifier and E (Economic) component allow us to cross these silos — revealing substations where low digital resilience intersects with high economic consequence. This is precisely the anticipatory intelligence that Costa Rica's Ley 8454 (2005) + National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023 (transposed via the EEA process, in force 1 January 2026) and MICITT (ECOI) competent-authority designation require, with CSIRT-CR as the operational CSIRT.
