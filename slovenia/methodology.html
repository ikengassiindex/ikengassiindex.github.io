Master Formula

For each substation i:

Ri = ( Ci·wC + Vi·wV + Ii·wI + Ei·wE + Si·wS + Ti·wT ) · R3i · R4i · R6_restorationi · R6_seismici · R7_cyberi

Components C, V, I, E, S, T are normalised to [0, ceiling] with weights summing to 1.0. Modifiers are multiplicative perturbations centred near 1.0 (range ~0.90 to ~1.12 per substation). Monte Carlo: 10,000 Gaussian-copula iterations per substation over a 20×20 correlation matrix yields R_median, R_P5, R_P95, CI_width.

R3 Consequence Multiplier — 3-Tier (Slovenia)

  • Capital-Intensive (1.02) — Osrednjeslovenska (Ljubljana metro): capital + financial + government concentration. 1 region.
  • Industrial-Major (1.04) — Podravska (Maribor), Savinjska (Celje), Gorenjska (Kranj), Obalno-kraška (Koper). 4 regions.
  • Light-Rural (1.06) — Pomurska, Koroška, Zasavska, Posavska, Jugovzhodna Slovenija, Primorsko-notranjska, Goriška. 7 regions.

Distribution 1 / 4 / 7 mirrors the Czech archetype (Praha + 4 industrial + 9 rural), preserving the small-EU 3-tier calibration shape.

R6 Hazard Calibration (Slovenia)

R6_seismic α ∈ [0.04, 0.14]

SE edge of Alpine seismic belt + Pannonian basin interface. HIGH (0.10-0.14): Posavska, Jugovzhodna, Goriška, Obalno-kraška (Krško zone + Idrija fault + 1976 Friuli spillover + 1998 Bovec M5.6). MEDIUM (0.06-0.09): Osrednjeslovenska, Gorenjska, Primorsko-notranjska. LOW (0.04-0.05): Pomurska, Podravska, Koroška, Savinjska, Zasavska. ZAG-EQS PGA 475-yr maps: Bovec/Tolmin/Idrija ~0.275 g, Krško ~0.20 g, Ljubljana ~0.225 g.

R6c_flood α ∈ [0.04, 0.10]

Defining anchor: August 2023 Savinja basin floods (~€10B damages, 7 fatalities, ~6,000 buildings — most severe modern SI disaster). HIGH (0.08-0.10): Savinjska, Osrednjeslovenska, Podravska, Pomurska. MEDIUM (0.05-0.07): Koroška, Zasavska, Posavska, Jugovzhodna, Goriška, Obalno-kraška. LOW (0.04): Gorenjska (Alpine), Primorsko-notranjska (karst).

R6_storm α ∈ [0.03, 0.06]

Reference event: February 2014 ice storm (žled) — ~10% of national grid down in Notranjska/Postojna corridor for days/weeks; ELES + DSOs cite as calibration anchor. Ice storm: Primorsko-notranjska, Goriška, Notranjska karst (0.05-0.06). Bora (burja) wind: Obalno-kraška, Primorsko-notranjska (0.05-0.06). Alpine convective: Gorenjska, Koroška (0.04). Continental thunderstorm: Pomurska, Podravska, Savinjska (0.03).

R6_restoration α ∈ [0.00, 0.03]

Alpine valley access penalty: Gorenjska (Triglav-Bovec), Goriška (upper Soča) — single-road valleys, α 0.02-0.03. Moderate: Koroška, Posavska, Jugovzhodna (0.01). Standard access elsewhere (0.00).

R7 Cyber Ceiling = 1.04

Slovenia has mature cyber posture for its size: SI-CERT founded 1995 (one of the oldest CERTs in CEE, peer of CERT-PL + CERT-CZ); URSIV established 2020 (government cyber coordination); NIS2 transposed Q4 2024 (ZInfV-1 amendment); DESI 2024 ~0.55 (mid-pack EU, above CZ, below DE). Reference incident: 2019 Triglav ransomware (insurance, not grid). R7 ceiling 1.04 aligns SI with CZ and AT (one notch below DE/CH at 1.06, one notch above LV/LT at 1.02-1.03).

Krško NPP Treatment

The Krško NPP (NEK) is a unique structural feature of the Slovenian grid: 696 MW Westinghouse PWR commissioned 1981, 50/50 GEN Energija (SI) / HEP (HR) ownership, life-extended through 2043 via URSJV Periodic Safety Review. Output split 50/50 between SI and HR consumption. The SSI applies R6_seismic α 0.10-0.12 to Posavska (Krško zone) to reflect compound hazard; URSJV oversight is treated as a continuous data source (quarterly summaries). JEK 2 / Krško 2 (proposed 1100 MW second reactor) is not yet in the asset register; EIA in progress, earliest commissioning 2037.
