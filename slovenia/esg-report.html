ESG Report — Slovenia 🇸🇮

8-section ESG impact assessment of the SSI Index Slovenia coverage · Edition 01

1. Overview — SSI Index in the Slovenian Context

The SSI Index applies a transparent, public-data-only methodology to Slovenia's 158 in-scope substations across 12 NUTS-3 statistical regions. Slovenia joined the OECD on 21 July 2010, the EU on 1 May 2004, and the Eurozone on 1 January 2007. The SSI publication aligns with the country's NEPN climate plan, the 2024 ZInfV-1 NIS2 transposition, and the EU Just Transition Fund commitments tied to the 2033 TEŠ-6 lignite phase-out.

2. Vulnerable Populations

Slovenia's vulnerable populations concentrate in three NUTS-3 regions with elevated energy-poverty rates: Pomurska (easternmost, lowest GDP/cap, agricultural dependency), Koroška (mountainous, sparse, ageing demographics), and Zasavska (legacy coal community in transition). The SSI's S component captures household-level vulnerability via SURS energy-poverty data, elderly population share, and unemployment rate per NUTS-3 region. The 2023 Savinja floods disproportionately affected Savinjska's rural communities — restoration timelines of 6-10 weeks in worst-hit valleys created sustained outage burdens on vulnerable households.

3. Economic Impact

The SSI's E (Economic) component and R3 (consequence multiplier) translate substation outages into estimated regional GDP impact. Slovenia's economy is concentrated in Osrednjeslovenska (Ljubljana, R3 1.02 Capital-Intensive) with secondary industrial centres in Maribor / Celje / Kranj / Koper (R3 1.04 Industrial-Major). Key sectoral exposures: KRKA pharmaceuticals + Revoz/Renault auto (Novo mesto, Jugovzhodna), Luka Koper port (Obalno-kraška), TEŠ-6 lignite + HSE generation (Šoštanj-Velenje, Savinjska), Krško NPP (Posavska, 50/50 SI-HR). 2024 nominal GDP ~€63B; GDP/capita ~€34,000 nominal / ~$53,000 PPP — mid-pack EU.

4. Climate Risk Exposure

Slovenia's climate signature is dominated by three hazards. (a) Flood — defining anchor August 2023 Savinja basin floods (~€10B damages, most severe modern disaster); R6c α 0.04-0.10 across 12 regions. (b) Seismic — Alpine + Pannonian interface; PGA peaks NW Soča valley + Krško NPP zone; R6 α 0.04-0.14. (c) Storm — ice storm (žled) NW/central, Bora (burja) Adriatic; February 2014 ice storm took ~10% of grid offline for days/weeks. Copernicus ERA5 + CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 climate scenarios feed forward-looking R6_climate adjustments.

5. Governance & Regulation

Regulatory oversight: AGEN-RS (Agencija za energijo) for tariffs, quality-of-supply, market oversight. URSJV (Uprava RS za jedrsko varnost) for Krško NPP safety. SI-CERT (founded 1995) + URSIV (established 2020) for cyber. URSZR (Uprava za zaščito in reševanje) for civil protection. ENTSO-E Continental Europe synchronous area; single bidding zone (10YSI-ELES-----O); BSP SouthPool / Borzen as NEMO. NIS2 transposed via ZInfV-1 amendment Q4 2024.

6. Social Equity & Just Transition

Slovenia's just-transition focus is concentrated in two regions: Savinjska (TEŠ-6 lignite phase-out by 2033, ~3,500 direct jobs at TEŠ + Premogovnik Velenje mine, ~€500M Just Transition Fund commitments) and Zasavska (legacy Trbovlje-Hrastnik coal closed 2014, ongoing economic transition). The SSI applies these as "transition watch-list" regions for future R3 re-tier sensitivity analysis; R7 cyber modifier unaffected.

7. Environmental Impact

ISO 9223 corrosion classes drawn from EEA AQ + ARSO atmospheric chemistry: C2 rural/mountain (Gorenjska Alpine, Koroška, much of Goriška), C3 urban (Ljubljana, Maribor, Celje, Novo mesto, Kranj), C4 industrial (Šoštanj-Velenje, Jesenice steel, Krško NPP zone, Trbovlje legacy coal), C5 coastal (46.6 km Adriatic coast — Obalno-kraška only; Koper port + Piran + Portorož + Izola; salt aerosol + SO₂ from shipping). C5 zone has highest asset-degradation rates per SSI corrosion-adjusted Markov chain.

8. Energy Transition

2024 generation mix: Nuclear ~36% (Krško 50% share = 348 MW SI), Hydro ~28% (HSE Drava + Soča + Sava cascades), Coal/lignite ~21% (TEŠ-6 sole remaining unit, 600 MW, 2033 phase-out), Gas/CHP ~12%, Solar ~3% (~750 MWp end-2024, rapid Eko sklad growth), Wind <0.1% (5 MW Dolenja vas only). NEPN 2024 target: 33% RES by 2030 (raised from 27%). JEK 2 / Krško 2 proposed (1100 MW second reactor, EIA in progress, parliamentary decision 2027-2028, earliest commissioning 2037). EV adoption ~3% end-2024 (lagging EU). Heat pumps ~12% of households.
