Section D

Deep-Dive: Savinjska — The 2023 Flood Recovery Corridor

In August 2023, the Savinja basin flooded after 200+ mm of rainfall in 12 hours; ~€10 billion in damages, 7 fatalities, ~6,000 buildings damaged or destroyed. Grid impact: ~120 MV substations + 18 HV substations affected; full restoration took 6–10 weeks in worst-hit valleys. The event re-anchored Slovenia's flood-modifier baseline: Savinjska R6c α now 0.10 (HIGH tier ceiling), and the broader Sava+Drava+Mura HIGH cluster sits at 0.08-0.09.

Savinjska also hosts the Šoštanj-Velenje lignite complex (TEŠ-6, 600 MW, ~21% of national electricity generation, ~3-4 Mt CO₂/year — largest single emitter in SI). NEPN 2024 reaffirmed the 2033 phase-out target with caveats; the closure removes the 600 MW from the SI generation mix and shifts flow patterns through the Šoštanj 400 kV transmission anchor. Just-Transition Fund commitments ~€500M tied to the NEPN closure date.

Section E

European Context

Slovenia sits in the ENTSO-E Continental Europe synchronous area, with one bidding zone (10YSI-ELES-----O) coupled to AT, IT, HR via SDAC + SIDC. The 2022 commissioning of the Cirkovce-Žerjavinec 400 kV line (SI-HR) was a major capacity boost; combined with the older Krško-Tumbri 400 kV line and the 50/50 NEK ownership split, Slovenia and Croatia operate one of the most tightly-coupled cross-border electricity systems in the EU.

Within the CEE-South-2026 cohort, Slovenia (2.1M pop, 12 NUTS-3, ~158 in-scope substations) is the smallest. Slovakia (next) brings 5.4M / 8 kraje / ~1,400 substations; Hungary (last) closes the cohort with 9.6M / 19+1 megyék / ~2,500 substations. Together with the live Czech + Polish coverage, CEE-South-2026 completes the Visegrád-4 (V4) pattern and brings EU coverage to 26 of 27 member states (Cyprus, Malta remaining).
