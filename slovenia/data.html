Methodology Preview

SSI v4.0.2 computes a per-substation risk score R as a function of six components (C, V, I, E, S, T) and five modifiers (R3 consequence multiplier, R4 topology, R6 restoration, R6 seismic, R7 cyber). Each substation runs 10,000 Gaussian Copula Monte Carlo iterations over a 20×20 correlation matrix; outputs include R_median, 5/95 percentile bounds, CI width, classification band, and component-level alerts. Full methodology in methodology.html.
