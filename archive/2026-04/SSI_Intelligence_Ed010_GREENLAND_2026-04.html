The SSI Index's Greenland Edition 001 is the first scored pass over a publicly-verifiable fleet. Session 1 comprises 37 substations, 74 plants / generators and 125 power-line segments — every asset geolocated from OpenStreetMap (extract 2026-04-16), with Pituffik Space Base (U.S. Navy / Space Force / Air Force operator, lat 75.5°–77.5°N, lon −71°…−66°) excluded by policy (see methodology §7). No synthetic nodes, no inferred cross-settlement edges: Greenland's grid is a set of isolated within-island networks and the topology reflects that exactly. The Avannaata coverage gap previously flagged at bootstrap was closed by direct OSM Overpass retrieval of node/11958572184 (Ilulissat transformer, 69.2205°N, −51.1012°E, 10.5 kV).

Regional distribution of the scored fleet and Kommune-level R_median: Avannaata 1 (R = 0.868), Qeqertalik 12 (R = 0.797), Kujalleq 20 (R = 0.774), Qeqqata 3 (R = 0.677), Sermersooq 1 (R = 0.655). The north-to-south gradient reflects Arctic restoration penalty (R6a α ∈ [0.55, 0.90]), permafrost-degradation infrastructure wear, CAIDI ≈ 8h (vs Nordic ~2h), and socio-economic vulnerability from Stat.gl 2024 (elderly %, unemployment, GDP/capita). Sermersooq scores best because Nuuk is anchored by 45 MW Buksefjord hydro and the metro load concentration lowers R3 saturation.

Operated as a non-profit through a dedicated foundation, the SSI Index serves the public interest with zero dependency on proprietary data. All 95 variables are drawn from 25+ verified public sources (Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur, Grønlands Statistik, Asiaq, GEUS, OpenStreetMap, CMIP6, Open-Meteo). The §8 Small-N guardrail runs the Monte Carlo at 1,000 iterations per substation (37,000 total draws) with components perturbed independently — the full 95-dimensional Gaussian copula is held off at N=37 because empirical correlation matrices inferred from 37 observations are numerically unstable. The copula is scheduled to activate once the Nukissiorfiit asset register supplements OSM and N crosses 500.

Section C — Recurring Module

Cyber & Economic Exposure Monitor

Why this section exists: Traditional grid risk frameworks treat cybersecurity and economic vulnerability as separate domains. The SSI's R7 (Digital Readiness) modifier and E (Economic) component cross these silos — identifying substations where low digital resilience intersects with high socio-economic consequence. This is precisely the anticipatory intelligence that Nukissiorfiit and Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur strategic planning will require.

C.1 — Cyber-Physical Risk Matrix

R7 digital-readiness modifier vs overall R_median. At Edition 001 R7 is calibrated against the ENISA NIS2 small-utility cyber baseline (V1 ≈ 0.65) because Greenland Connect's 2024 broadband coverage and Nukissiorfiit's OT segmentation are both weak signals on a 37-node fleet. R7 is uniformly in the [1.04, 1.08] band across substations — a flat digital penalty. The blind-spot quadrant (high R_median · high R7 penalty) therefore tracks R_median directly: every substation above 0.85 R_median doubles as a cyber-exposure candidate.

Top blind-spot candidates: the 29 Critical-band substations — with Avannaata (Ilulissat transformer) R=0.868 as the single highest-risk node. Full matrix will expand once Nukissiorfiit smart-meter penetration and OT segmentation indicators ingest at Edition 002.

C.2 — Economic Impact by Industrial Sector

R3 consequence segmentation, Edition 001: Hydro & heavy industry — Buksefjord-fed Sermersooq nodes (1), R3 ≈ 1.18. Commercial & Nuuk metro — Sermersooq/Qeqqata settlement clusters (4), R3 ≈ 1.15. Settlement — Kujalleq + Qeqertalik micro-grids (31), R3 ≈ 1.25 (elderly % 13–16, unemployment 11–13%, GDP/cap DKK 205–235k). Rural / remote — Avannaata Ilulissat isolated transformer (1), R3 ≈ 1.28.

VoLL range (indicative, 2024 DKK/MWh): hydro & heavy 22–40k · commercial 11–16k · settlement 6–9k · rural 3–5k. Greenland's R3 band overall is compressed high because every kommune is below the pan-OECD median for socio-economic resilience.

Average R7 by Kommune (lower R7 = better digital readiness ⇒ smaller penalty multiplier): Sermersooq 1.04 (Nuuk fibre + smart-meter pilots) · Qeqqata 1.05 · Qeqertalik 1.06 · Kujalleq 1.06 · Avannaata 1.07. Cross-referencing against R_median produces the digital-exposure composite:

Section E

Deep-Dive: The Buksefjord Corridor

The Buksefjord hydropower complex (45 MW, commissioned 1993, expanded 2007) feeding Nuuk via a 132 kV submarine-plus-overhead transmission is the most strategically critical single asset in Greenland's grid. It is the only Greenlandic installation where R4 graph criticality can plausibly rise into the Critical band under scoring — Nuuk has no realistic alternative supply path and any Buksefjord interruption places the capital on full diesel backup. At the same time, its Atlantic-facing corridor exposes the transmission right-of-way to polar-low storms, ice accretion, and freezing salt spray — hazards that the R6a restoration modifier (α ∈ [0.55, 0.90]) and the R6b Arctic storm overlay are calibrated to capture.

Because Sermersooq only contains 1 OSM-tagged substation in the Edition 001 fleet — Buksefjord itself or its immediate downstream node — the SSI cannot yet quantify corridor-wide cascade risk. Edition 001 records the Sermersooq node at R_median = 0.655 (High band) — the best performer in the fleet, reflecting the Buksefjord anchor and Nuuk-metro load concentration. Future editions will rely on Nukissiorfiit to supply the complete Nuuk-ring topology (Qinngorput, Nuussuaq, downtown, and the Qaquliaasaq tie) before the corridor deep-dive can be replaced with a full R4 + R6b heat-band analysis. For Edition 001 this section remains a methodological marker: the corridor is where the R6a Arctic restoration calibration and the §7 Pituffik exclusion policy were originally stress-tested.

Data Architecture — Component Taxonomy

How the SSI's 6 components and 20 sub-metrics are sourced, validated and scored for Greenland's grid

The SSI-ENN's Layer 1 formally specifies the SSI v4.0.2 Index for Greenland — its six components (Continuity, Voltage, Infrastructure, Economic, Saturation, Transition), twenty sub-metrics, normalisation methodology and aggregation rules. Each sub-metric is independently sourced from one of the 25+ public data sources in the Greenlandic SSI registry (Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur, Grønlands Statistik, Asiaq, GEUS), validated against known reference distributions, and normalised to a common [0, 1] scale before aggregation. Edition 001 completes the first end-to-end ingest pass: all 95 fields populated, all 37 substations scored.

→Each sub-metric traceable to Nukissiorfiit, Departementet for Boliger og Infrastruktur or an open data source
