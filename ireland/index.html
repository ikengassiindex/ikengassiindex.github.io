The First Open Digital Twin Fusing Grid Risk with Societal Exposure

A composite resilience score for 1,278 Irish substations — 58 EHV (≥220 kV), 268 HV (110–220 kV), 952 distribution-tier (<110 kV or untagged) — 101 variables, 34 public data sources, 11 modifiers (5 baseline + 6 v4.2 resilience), Re composite bounded [0.920, 1.787], zero proprietary dependencies.

Operated as a non-profit through a dedicated foundation — ensuring the Index serves the public interest.
Breaking the Silos

Power grid resilience is inseparable from its socio-economic context. The SSI fuses traditional grid metrics with socio-economic, environmental, and energy-transition data — all from public sources — to capture what single-discipline indices miss.

Competitive Positioning

SSI v4.2 extends the v4.0.2 baseline (15 of 16 dimensions vs CEER, CIGRÉ/IEEE, EPRI, DOE/PNNL GMLC, JRC DSO Observatory, and academic MCDA) with the Re composite, six resilience modifiers (R6c flood / R6d wildfire / R6e winter / R8 adapt / R9 compound / R10 just), and the W1–W10 anti-maladaptation governance gate.
