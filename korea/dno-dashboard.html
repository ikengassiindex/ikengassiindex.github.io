DSO Dashboard — KEPCO Single-DSO Monopoly 🇰🇷

Korea is the cohort-FIRST single-DSO monopoly country in OECD coverage. KEPCO (Korea Electric Power Corporation, 한국전력공사) holds 100% of transmission + distribution + retail electricity sales across the entire peninsula.
