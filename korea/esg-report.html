ESG Report — Republic of Korea 🇰🇷

Environmental, Social, and Governance assessment of Korea's electrical grid resilience — 8 sections covering climate, multi-hazard (typhoon + induced seismic), nuclear (24 reactors), cyber-leadership (cohort-best 25-yr KISA), social-economic chaebol concentration, and governance (single-DSO monopoly architectural test).

🌍 R1 — Environmental: Climate & Multi-Hazard

Korean Peninsula temperate-monsoon + typhoon-cyclone climate. CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 projects intensifying summer heat (2018 record summer anchor) + intensifying typhoon (Hinnamnor 2022 + Khanun 2023). C5 corrosion class RESTORED for peninsula-full marine coast (parallel to Iceland post-CEE-South-2026). Sub-Arctic transformer headroom benefit (×1.10 factor) does NOT apply — Korean Peninsula is temperate.

R6_typhoon NEW modifier (α ∈ [0.03, 0.15]) — UNIQUE in cohort with Iceland's R6_volcanic. Anchors: Hinnamnor 2022 (66,000 lost power · Geoje landfall), Khanun 2023 (40,350 lost), Bolaven 2012 (Jeju historical).

🌋 R1.5 — Environmental: Pohang 2017 Induced Earthquake Ethics

Pohang Mw 5.5 earthquake (15 November 2017) was the largest + most damaging modern Korean seismic event. Anthropogenically induced — EGS (Enhanced Geothermal System) hydraulic injection triggered fault rupture (Grigoli et al., Science, 2018). Mainshock within 2 km of EGS site. Korean court 2019 ruled the geothermal project responsible. ~135 injured, ~1,800 buildings damaged.

ESG significance: Korea's grid resilience model must account for anthropogenic seismic risk from energy infrastructure (geothermal injection sites, CO2 sequestration). R6b α=0.10 for N. Gyeongsang reflects this. Sets precedent for next-cohort R6_induced sub-pattern.

👥 R2 — Social: Aging Demographic + Chaebol Concentration

Korea has cohort-elevated elderly demographic — 22-26% over-65 in agricultural Jeolla/Gyeongsang provinces vs 11.5% in Sejong (youngest in cohort). Energy-poverty risk concentrated in mountainous Gangwon (interior C2) + rural Jeolla/Gyeongsang.

R6_chaebol NEW modifier (α ∈ [0.00, 0.10]) — Samsung Pyeongtaek + SK Hynix Icheon + LG Paju + POSCO Gwangyang/Pohang + Hyundai Ulsan represent ~50% national GDP + ~40% national electricity. Single-site catastrophic loss potential exceeds Iceland aluminum corridor (3 sites). Ethical question: does R3 alone (1.05 max for Industrial) capture chaebol-driven concentration risk? Korea's answer: NO — R6_chaebol added as separate magnitude-of-impact modifier.

🏭 R4 — Industrial Transition: 11th Basic Plan 2025

Korea's 2024 generation mix: 32% nuclear · 30% coal · 28% LNG · <6.5% renewables · 1.5% hydro. Nuclear surpassed coal in 2024 for first time. MOTIE 11th Basic Plan (Feb 2025) target: 35.2% nuclear + 29.7% RES + 10.1% coal + 10.6% LNG by 2038. Kori 2 restarted April 2026 — first Korean reactor with 40-yr operating-lifetime extension granted. 9 reactors pending NSSC review (Kori 3-4, Hanbit 1-2, Hanul 1-2, Wolsong 2-4).

🌊 R5 — Corrosion: ISO 9223 C5 RESTORED

Korean Peninsula is fully marine (peninsular geography). C5 corrosion class applies to all coastal do/si: Jeju + S. Jeolla coast + S. Gyeongsang coast + Busan + Pohang coast + Incheon + Gangwon east coast. Follows Iceland post-CEE-South-2026 pattern (C5 returned after HU/SK landlocked C2-C4 only). Interior Gangwon highlands + interior Chungbuk remain C2.

🔐 R6 — Cyber: Cohort-Best 25-Yr KISA + KrCERT

Korea has cohort-LEADING cyber maturity. KISA (Korea Internet & Security Agency) + KrCERT/CC founded 2001 — 25-year incident catalogue (vs HU GovCERT 2008, IS CERT-IS 2013). Act on the Protection of Information and Communications Infrastructure (정보통신기반보호법) since 2001-12-26 — pre-dates EU NIS2 by 22 years. NIS coordination via NIS (National Intelligence Service).

R7 ceiling 1.015 — cohort-LOWEST. Reflects mature cyber regime. C-TAS (Cyber Threat Analysis and Sharing) platform operational since 2014.

⚖️ R7 — Governance: Single-DSO Monopoly Architectural Test

Korea is the first OECD country in cohort with single-DSO monopoly — KEPCO holds ALL transmission + distribution + retail across the entire peninsula. TSO function splits: KPX (non-profit market+system operator) + KEPCO (asset owner). Governance flag is_kepco_monopoly: true triggers single-DSO panel rendering on dno-dashboard.html. NEW architectural test surfaces for the cohort.

Cohort governance comparison:
