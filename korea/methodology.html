Methodology

How the SSI v4.0.2 is calculated — from raw data to final classification. Open methodology, open data, fully reproducible.

Breaking the Silos

Power grid resilience is inseparable from its socio-economic context. A do/si serving an energy-poor region with slow restoration times and weak fiscal capacity is fundamentally more vulnerable than one with identical electrical characteristics in a prosperous area. The SSI captures this reality by fusing traditional grid metrics with socio-economic, environmental, and energy-transition data — all from public sources.

Processing Pipeline
1

Ingest

95 variables from 19 verified public data sources — KPX/KEPCO network statement, KOREC quality reports, KEPCO single-DSO filings, KOSTAT regional data, Bank of Korea national accounts, KMA meteorology, NEMA flood disaster mapping, KIGAM seismic + KMA, + NSSC nuclear safety, KrCERT/CC + Cyber Security Authority (KISA-affiliated) cyber, Copernicus, ENTSO-E, Eurostat, OSM Overpass. Zero proprietary SCADA dependencies. Maximum ingestion frequency: daily.

2

Normalise

Four normalisation methods: Method A (robust fleet percentile P5/P95), Method B (standard fleet percentile), Method C (bounded rescaling), and Method D (categorical mapping). Inverted metrics for density measures where higher = better resilience.

3

Weight

6-level hierarchical weighting across 6 components and 20 metrics. Continuity dominates at 0.30, followed by Infrastructure (0.25) and Saturation (0.20). Weight budget validated by Sobol sensitivity analysis.

4

Compose R_base

Weighted sum of 6 normalised component scores produces the base resilience score.

5

Modify

Six multiplicative modifiers adjust R_base for context: R2 (adaptive climate IRI), R3 (consequence + energy poverty — Korea uses a 4-tier calibration reflecting chaebol-concentration: Industrial-Chaebol 1.05 / Capital-Seoul 1.04 / SME 1.03 / Rural 1.02), R4 (graph-theoretic network criticality), R6a (restoration speed — KEPCO single-DSO CAIDI; Jeju isolated HVDC penalty), R6b (network topology), R7 (digital readiness, ceiling 1.015 — cohort-LOWEST · KISA 2001 25-yr backlog).

6

Monte Carlo

10,000 iterations per do/si using a 20×20 Gaussian copula correlation matrix. Captures measurement, spatial, and staleness uncertainty. Produces median, P5, P95, skewness, and P_critical for each do/si.

7

Classify

Four bands: Low (0.00–0.25), Medium (0.25–0.50), High (0.50–0.75), Critical (0.75–1.00). Alert flags trigger when any single component exceeds its P95 fleet threshold.

Uncertainty Quantification

Monte Carlo engine

Every do/si score includes a 90% confidence interval derived from 10,000 Monte Carlo iterations. The simulation uses a 20×20 Gaussian copula to preserve empirical correlations between metrics (e.g., ρ(C1, E1) ≈ 0.70–0.85). Three uncertainty sources are modelled: measurement error, spatial disaggregation noise, and data staleness.
